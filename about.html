Throughit

Throughit is a platform designed to help students manage their assignments and deadlines effectively.

The goal is to simplify the assignment management process, allowing students to focus on their studies rather than getting overwhelmed by deadlines.

  • Why the name Throughit?

    Throughit serves as a reminder that even in the challenging tasks if we push through we'll eventually get the work done.If you believe and persevere you will get THROUGH IT!

    Why Throughit?

    As a student who struggles with getting started with my assignments, I chose to work on this to help myself and many others minimise time-blindess by having a personal deadline and a timer. The idea of a clarity pledge helps in keeping the student on toes as they have a personal goal to achieve.
